Put on headphones. Tap start to hear a normal heart at the reference level, then set your device volume so it is comfortably audible.

Set your volume

A normal heart at 72 bpm is playing at the tool's reference level. Every sound in Gallop is relative to this, and Gallop never changes it. Adjust your device volume until it is comfortable, then continue.

Can you hear this?

A 40 Hz tone is playing at the reference level. This is the bottom of the bell's range.

The low end is missing

This device is not reproducing bell-range sounds (about 20 to 70 Hz). S3, S4 and low rumbles will be inaudible here. That is the device, not your ears. You can still practise everything the diaphragm hears. With headphones, start again and the bell comes back.
